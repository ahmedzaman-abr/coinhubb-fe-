It usually starts with a screenshot. Someone's cousin turned a small amount into a big one, the chart only goes up, and there's a link to a "VIP signals" group. By dinner, three relatives want to know if they should put their savings in.

You don't need to be an expert to be the calm person in that chat. You need three ideas and one rule.

## 1. Anything that guarantees returns is lying

Real markets go down as well as up. A fixed "daily profit" is the signature of a scheme that pays early people with later people's money.

<aside class="gloss">
<strong>Ponzi scheme</strong>
<p>A setup where "profits" come from new investors' deposits, not from anything actually earning money. It works until new money slows down, then it collapses.</p>
</aside>
